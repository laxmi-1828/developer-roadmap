How to use the Master Prompts

      
    
        
  1. Choose your platform. Pick ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude — whichever you use most.
    2. 
        
  2. Open its Master Prompt above and read it once, so you know what it does.
    3. 
        
  3. Copy the complete prompt with the Copy button.
    4. 
        
  4. Add it to your AI tool as a saved system/custom instruction, project instruction, or the first message of a dedicated chat — whatever your platform supports.
    5. 
        
  5. Save that setup so you can reuse it any time you need a new prompt.
    6. 
        
  6. Describe what you want, e.g. “Create a prompt for ChatGPT to help me debug my Laravel project.”
    7. 
        
  7. Let the Master Prompt rebuild it into a structured, ready-to-use prompt.
    8. 
        
  8. Copy that optimized prompt, open a new chat in your target AI tool, and use it for the real task.
    9.

Best practices

      
    
        
  • Give enough context for the task at hand.
    • 
        
  • State your goal clearly.
    • 
        
  • Include real errors or examples when relevant.
    • 
        
  • Mention your technology or framework.
    • 
        
  • Say what output format you want.
    • 
        
  • Review every AI-generated answer.
    • 
        
  • Never share passwords, API keys, credentials or confidential information.
